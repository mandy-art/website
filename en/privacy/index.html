This policy explains which personal data are processed when you visit this website, use its tools or contact me. It applies to this website only.

No cookies, no tracking

This website does not use cookies, analytics, tracking tools or social media plugins. Fonts and images are stored on the website itself, so no data are sent to font or image services when you open a page.

Hosting

This website is hosted by statichost.eu, Stockholm, Sweden. When you open a page, the hosting provider technically processes your IP address, the date and time of access, the page requested and information about your browser. This is necessary to deliver the website and keep it secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). I do not use these data to identify you. The provider processes them on my behalf, using servers and services located in the European Union.

Language choice

When you choose a language on this website, your choice is saved in your browser's local storage so that the website opens in the same language on your next visit. This setting stays on your device and is not sent to me or to anyone else. You can delete it at any time by clearing the website data in your browser.

When you contact me

If you write to me by email or WhatsApp, I process the information you send me, such as your name, contact details and your message, in order to reply to you and, where relevant, to prepare our work together (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). My emails are handled by Google Workspace. WhatsApp is a service of WhatsApp Ireland Ltd., and its own privacy policy applies when you use it. I delete your messages when they are no longer needed, unless legal retention periods apply.

Tools on this website

The tools on this website, such as the Meeting Cost Clock or Body Signals, run entirely in your browser. Entries and settings, for example your chosen language, are at most stored in your browser's local storage. So that the tools also work offline, your browser may additionally store their files. No data are sent to me or to anyone else. You can delete all stored data at any time by clearing the website data in your browser or removing an installed app from your home screen.

External links

This website links to LinkedIn and to the website of Beruf und Gesundheit e.V. No data are transferred to them unless you click on the link. After that, their privacy policies apply.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct and delete your personal data, to restrict or object to their processing and to receive them in a portable format. To use these rights, write to me at the email address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Spanish data protection authority, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos.
