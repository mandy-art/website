Healthy leadership for resilient organisations

Coaching & Leadership Development

Coaching and development programmes for leaders and teams, aimed at healthy leadership and resilient organisations. The focus is on decision-making, collaboration and the psychosocial health of leaders and teams.

Services

Executive Coaching

One-to-one coaching for leaders on decisions, feedback, conflict and the demands of their role.

Team Diagnostics & Development

An assessment of how the team works together, a joint review of the results in a workshop, and support for the team over several months.

Leadership Programmes & Retreats

Programmes for groups of leaders, some of them on Fuerteventura with coaching walks outdoors.

Advisory for Senior Leadership

Leadership development and collaboration between leaders across functions and cultures.

Beruf und Gesundheit e.V.

Associate partner of Beruf und Gesundheit e.V., a network of specialists in Germany who support companies and individuals with questions of health, coaching and HR. The focus within the network is healthy leadership, meaning the mental health of leaders themselves and the effect of their leadership on the health of their people.
