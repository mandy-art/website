Interim Management

Taking on leadership and management responsibilities for a defined period when organisations are growing, restructuring or being brought together. The work addresses structures and processes as much as leadership and collaboration.

Areas of focus

Interim Chief of Staff

Taking load off the executive team, managing priorities and the interfaces between strategy, operations and HR.

Transformation & Programme Leadership

Global programmes, standardisation and restructuring, with attention to the strain on the workforce.

Post-Merger Integration

Bringing together organisations, leadership teams and cultures.
